01. Keep the DNA.

Square corners, warm paper, 3px ink, hard shadows. The personality is in the structure.

02. Make it yours.

Swap the accent, content, and product images. Keep the component roles stable.

03. Ship the boring bits.

Labels, focus, loading, stock, errors. Clear beats clever when money moves.

This is a component playground, not a finished store. Product names, prices, and order details are examples.
